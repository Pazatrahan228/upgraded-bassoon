# Fresh Vibes DSD Studio Roadmap

## P0 — must-have

- Real photo upload with storage.
- Real patient and case database.
- Real PDF export pipeline.
- Connect Supabase auth, database, and storage.
- Protect doctor/admin routes.
- Add production deployment environment variables.

## P1 — important

- AI smile detection and calibrated landmark extraction.
- Editable overlay points and saved guide positions.
- Doctor comments and internal review workflow.
- Patient approval flow with timestamps.
- Treatment pricing and estimate integration.
- Audit trail for exported reports and approvals.

## P2 — future premium features

- 3D STL viewer and dental arch comparison.
- AI treatment suggestions with clinical guardrails.
- Multi-clinic support and role permissions.
- WhatsApp/email sharing for patient presentations.
- Advanced analytics for case acceptance and revenue.
- Doctor signature, patient signature, and consent templates.
